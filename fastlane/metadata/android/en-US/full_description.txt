SpectrumAnalyzer2 is an Android application for visualizing and analyzing audio frequencies.

Features:

* Real-time audio spectrum visualization.
* Waterfall spectrogram for viewing frequency changes over time.
* Interactive cursor selection in the spectrum graph.
* Frequency and decibel (dB) information at the selected position.
* Adjustable spectrum display and frequency range.
* Landscape-oriented interface for convenient analysis.

SpectrumAnalyzer2 is open source and released under the MIT license.
