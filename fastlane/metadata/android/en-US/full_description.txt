SpectrumAnalyzer2 is an Android app for real-time audio analysis, sound identification, and tone generation.

AUDIO ANALYSIS
* View live audio as a spectrum or waterfall spectrogram.
* Switch between linear and logarithmic frequency scales.
* Zoom and pan the graph horizontally across frequencies and vertically through the dB range.
* Select a point on the graph to view its frequency and level.
* Adjust the frequency range from 20 Hz to 20 kHz and use peak hold.
* View an estimated SPL level with reference calibration.

SOUND ID
* Identify sounds offline with the bundled YAMNet model and its 521 sound classes.
* View the leading match and alternative predictions.
* Review a session summary showing which sounds were detected over time.
* Export session summaries as PDF.

MY SOUND ID
* Record 5- or 10-second samples, name them, and save them for future matching.
* Play, pause, rename, or delete your recordings.
* Import WAV samples and import or export ZIP archives containing your samples and profiles.

TONE GENERATOR
* Generate sine, square, triangle, and sawtooth tones.
* Create white noise, pink noise, and DTMF tones.
* Use frequency presets, follow the detected peak, or add your own presets.
* Run frequency sweeps in either direction for 4, 8, 16, or 32 seconds.
* Choose LEFT, RIGHT, or STEREO audio output.

PIANO KEYBOARD
* Play a multi-touch piano keyboard with volume, octave, and reverb controls.
* Expand the keyboard to full-screen mode.
